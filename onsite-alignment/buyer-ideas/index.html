Five buyer ideas, on the live shop

Each idea comes from the buyer journey deep dive (How Buyers Buy) and sits on today's lumas.de pages, with production's own fonts, buttons, prices and product cards. The purchase stays one tap away in every idea; none of them sends anyone to a gallery.

Pages are captured from the live shop on first visit; nothing reaches the real shop (the cart is simulated). Add ?bi=none to any page in this copy to see production without the ideas.

Idea 1 · Homepage, returning visitors

Pick up where you left off

The homepage opens on the work the visitor configured last time: framed render, size and finish, today's price and one tap to the cart. The other works they looked at follow in production's carousel. Configure any work on a product page and come back to the homepage to see your own.

Why: 35% of net online revenue comes from buyers who had decided before the buying visit (16–22 Nov, lumas.de).

Open on desktopConfigure a work first

Measures: bi_return_view, bi_return_click, bi_add_to_cart (source return), bi_return_dismiss. Primary: net revenue per returning visitor.

Idea 2 · Product page

Send it to my phone

Under the buy button on desktop: "Auf dem Handy weiter ansehen" opens a QR code. Scanned, the product page opens on the phone with the same size and finish already set, and says so above the buy button. On the phone the same link reads "Am Computer weiter ansehen" and copies the link.

Why: 47% of buyers GA4 counts as first visits are existing customers on another device or browser.

Open on desktopWhat the phone opens

Measures: bi_handoff_open, bi_handoff_arrive, then purchases carrying bi-handoff.

Idea 3 · Gallery receipt to the website

Your edition, online

The gallery receipt and the order email carry a link to the artist's page, opened as "Deine Edition": the work the collector took home, when and where, and four works by the same artist chosen to hang beside it (one per other format, at a similar price level), as production's product cards. The rest of the artist page follows as it is.

Idea 5 · Homepage on phones

Artworks first on mobile

On phones the homepage opens on four bestsellers with their prices, taken from production's own bestseller carousel; the campaign hero and the rest of the page follow unchanged. Desktop is untouched.

Why: 53% of mobile visits reach a product page, against 68% on desktop. Up to €19k net a week at stake.

Open (narrow the window)

Measures: bi_entry_view, bi_entry_click (position); primary: share of mobile visits reaching a product page, then net revenue per visit.

Idea 6 · Every page after the first add to cart

Cart stays in sight

After the first add to cart, a compact bar keeps the work, its size and price and "Zur Kasse" in view while the visitor keeps looking. It slides away while scrolling down and comes back on the way up; on phones it sits above the bottom navigation, on desktop bottom right.

Why: between the first add to cart and the purchase, 34% of buyers looked at another artwork and 15% added a second work (16–22 Nov, lumas.de).

Open on desktop

Measures: bi_cartbar_view, bi_cartbar_click; primary: cart-to-purchase rate and multi-work orders on mobile.

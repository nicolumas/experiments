The archive

The picture editor’s cut

A shoot came back from the lab as a box of slides. Somebody set them out on a light table, leaned in with a loupe, and decided which frames the world would ever see. Everything else went back in the box.

These nine came out again with his own notes still on the mounts: places, names, years, box numbers. Each one is a small piece of photographic history.

“I photograph attractive people doing attractive things in attractive places.”

Slim Aarons

The particulars

Format

34 × 34 cm, square. Every edition is the same size, so any combination hangs on one grid.

Technique

Photographic print on an aluminium composite panel, finished with acrylic glass. The rounded corners take the form of a slide mount, a nod to analogue photography.

Edition

Limited edition per motif, carrying the Slim Aarons estate stamp.

Delivery

Framing options are shown on each work’s page. Ships in 5 to 7 days, 60-day returns.

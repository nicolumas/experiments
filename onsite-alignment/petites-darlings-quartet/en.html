FAQ

Good to know

How does the offer work?

Choose any four small-format works: Petites, Darlings or a mix of both. At checkout, the lowest-priced work in your four is deducted automatically. No code needed.

Can I mix Petites and Darlings?

Yes. Any four works make a quartet, and the offer applies across both collections.

Are frames included?

Every work arrives ready to hang with its specified framing. Darlings come with a passepartout and a white, brown or black frame. The details are on each work’s page.

What gift packaging is available?

Every Petite arrives in complimentary gift packaging. Darlings can be upgraded with a premium gift box in orange, purple or pink.

Three things the copy had to fix

The brandbook blacklists discount as a lead message and asks for price advantage or a named promotional window, which is why the confirmation line is worded the way it is. It also rules out exclamation marks, so Last Chance Today! could not stay. The third is structural: the price badge directly above the button already states the percentage, and the no-redundancy rule allows it only once per asset. The offer line here repeats it by decision, not by oversight.

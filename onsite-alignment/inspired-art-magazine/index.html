INNOVATIVE TECHNIQUES

New Dimensions in Art

The idea does not end with the image. When artistic vision, format and execution come together, new ways of experiencing art emerge: exceptional materials, precision craftsmanship and innovative techniques create distinctive works with a presence all their own.

Art in Motion – Multi Phase

Images that transform with every change in perspective, revealing a different effect from every angle: lenticular technology brings every facet of the artwork to life.

Learn more

Strings Attached – Art Made from Nails and Thread

In our String Art Editions, tension becomes an artistic principle – quite literally: a single thread stretching for metres is woven across thousands of nails, building into form and lettering and transforming the artwork into an extraordinary object with a tangible physical presence.

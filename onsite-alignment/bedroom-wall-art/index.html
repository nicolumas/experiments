Proportion

What actually works above a bed

100 – 120 cm

Two thirds

Aim for a work roughly two-thirds the width of the bed. Above a 160 cm bed, that's a work around 100 to 120 cm wide. Narrower than half and it reads as an afterthought.

20 – 25 cm

Hanging height

Leave 20 to 25 cm between the top of the headboard and the bottom of the frame. In a bedroom you hang lower than you would anywhere else, because the room is seen from lying down.

One · a pair · not a wall of five

One or several

One work is calmer and almost always safer. A pair reads as deliberate. Three or more starts to look like a gallery wall, which fights a room meant for rest.

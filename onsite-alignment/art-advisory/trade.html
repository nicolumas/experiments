Distinctive to LUMAS

The questions we are asked most often

Do you work to our brief, or impose a LUMAS look?

Yours. Your Art Consultant is briefed on your positioning, your audience and your interior scheme before any work is proposed. LUMAS has no house style to protect, we represent hundreds of artists precisely so that the answer can be different every time.

What is the minimum project size?

There isn't one that matters. We work from a single statement wall to a rolling programme across sites. The process is the same; only the scale of the brief changes.

Can we reorder the same works in a few years?

Your specification is held on file, so format, framing and glazing match exactly. Editions are limited and capped, so a specific work can sell out, we flag which of your placements are at risk at handover and hold reserve numbers where you ask us to.

Who handles installation, and when?

LUMAS-managed teams, scheduled around your operation, typically out of hours, room by room. You receive a signed handover sheet for each space.

How do you handle framing, safety and compliance?

One framing and glazing standard across the property, with anti-reflective glazing for daylight-facing walls. Security fixings are standard in public areas, and fire-classification documentation is issued on request, ask for it early rather than at handover.

Do you sell, lease or rotate?

Sale is standard, and the works become a documented asset on your register. Rental, rotating and phased-purchase structures are available for spaces that prefer the spend off the capital budget or like to refresh regularly.

Frequently asked questions

Is LUMAS right for my business?

If you place art in spaces people spend time in — hotels, offices, clinics, residences or retail — we can help. From a single statement wall to a rolling art programme across sites, we scale to the brief.

How does the process work?

A dedicated Art Consultant learns the space and the intent, proposes a considered selection with formats and framing, and — once you decide — handles production, delivery and installation. One point of contact from brief to wall.

How long does a project take?

It depends on scale and framing. A handful of pieces can be ready in a few weeks; a multi-site programme is planned to your timeline. We confirm lead times with every quote.

Do business clients get special terms?

Yes — trade partners work with dedicated project pricing and terms, with clear quotes and no surprises.

Do you offer leasing or rotating art?

We can arrange rotating and rental programmes for spaces that like to refresh their art regularly — ask your Art Consultant about the options for your project.

Can you deliver across multiple locations?

Yes. We coordinate production, logistics and professional installation across Europe and beyond, so a rollout across several sites stays consistent and on schedule.

Specification

The specification, in full

Editions, production, framing, safety, lead time and reordering, as they stand in the contract, before you ask for them.

Editions
Signed, numbered, limited. Certificate of authenticity per work, the same edition a private collector buys.
Formats
30 × 40 cm to 200 × 300 cm. Custom sizing on request for statement and lobby works.
Materials
Diasec® face-mount, museum-grade pigment print, or hand-varnished canvas.
Framing
Solid wood or aluminium, a single standard across the property. Anti-reflective glazing for daylight-facing walls.
Safety
Security fixings for public areas. Fire-classification documentation on request, normally required for newbuild sign-off.
Lead time
Typically six to ten weeks from curation sign-off, including installation scheduling.
Installation
LUMAS-managed, out of hours, room by room, with a signed handover sheet per space.
Reordering
Your specification held on file, replacements and phase two match exactly.

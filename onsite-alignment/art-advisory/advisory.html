What you get

What happens in those thirty minutes

  • The workA short selection chosen by hand for your room and your taste, with the reasoning behind each one.
  • The scaleThe right size for the wall, from petite editions to museum format, judged against the room rather than a thumbnail.
  • The frameFrame, mount and finish held against your interior, with the samples in hand.
  • The wallA visualisation of the work in your room, then framing, delivery and hanging handled for you.

Collector story

“I didn’t want a piece you eventually stop noticing. I wanted something I enjoy every single day.”

Natacha from Mannheim had long been drawn to the works of Erin Cone. Her daughter asked, “Why a faceless woman in the living room?” The answer came not in words, but in the space itself: a visualisation made visible what the artwork could become, and ultimately convinced everyone.

“It’s not just an aesthetic upgrade. It’s an investment in quality of life.”Natacha Waller, Mannheim, 2026

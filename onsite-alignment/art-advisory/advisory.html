Collector story

“I didn’t want a piece you eventually stop noticing. I wanted something I enjoy every single day.”

What began as a renovation became the moment to fulfil that wish. Natacha from Mannheim had long been drawn to the works of Erin Cone. Her daughter asked, “Why a faceless woman in the living room?” The answer came not in words, but in the space itself. A visualisation made visible what the artwork could become, and ultimately convinced everyone. Today, the piece is part of her everyday life: not something in the background, but something she returns to, every day. A piece of gallery feeling, at home.

“I can only encourage anyone considering bringing art into their home to do it. It’s not just an aesthetic upgrade. It’s an investment in quality of life.”Natacha Waller, Mannheim, 2026

Collector story

“I didn’t want a piece you eventually stop noticing — I wanted something I enjoy every single day.”

What began as a renovation became the moment to fulfil that wish. Natacha from Mannheim had long been drawn to the works of Erin Cone. The question her daughter asked — “Why a faceless woman in the living room?” — was answered not in words, but in the space itself. A visualisation made visible what the artwork could become, and ultimately convinced everyone. Today, the piece is part of her everyday life: not something in the background, but something she returns to, every day. A piece of gallery feeling, at home.

“I can only encourage anyone considering bringing art into their home to do it. It’s not just an aesthetic upgrade — it’s an investment in quality of life.”Natacha Waller, Mannheim, 2026

Reference

Four Seasons Gresham Palace, Budapest

A listed Art Nouveau building where nothing generic survives contact with the architecture. We curated per room type rather than per floor, matched the edition palette to the original Zsolnay tilework, and installed to one framing standard out of guest hours.

Distinctive to LUMAS

Guests can acquire the edition from the room it hangs in

A discreet in-room card links each edition to its page on lumas.com. Guests acquire it at gallery terms, and the property earns a referral share.

Because LUMAS represents the edition itself, nothing passes through a reseller: the work on the wall and the work for sale are one and the same.

Specification

The specification, in full

Editions, production, framing, safety, lead time and reordering, as they stand in the contract, before you ask for them.

Editions
Signed, numbered, limited. Certificate of authenticity per work, the same edition a private collector buys.
Formats
30 × 40 cm to 200 × 300 cm. Custom sizing on request for statement and lobby works.
Materials
Diasec® face-mount, museum-grade pigment print, or hand-varnished canvas.
Framing
Solid wood or aluminium, a single standard across the property. Anti-reflective glazing for daylight-facing walls.
Safety
Security fixings for public areas. Fire-classification documentation on request, normally required for newbuild sign-off.
Lead time
Typically six to ten weeks from curation sign-off, including installation scheduling.
Installation
LUMAS-managed, out of hours, room by room, with a signed handover sheet per space.
Reordering
Your specification held on file, replacements and phase two match exactly.

We are here to help

The questions we are asked most often

Do you work to our brief, or impose a LUMAS look?

Yours. Your Art Consultant is briefed on your positioning, guest profile and interior scheme before any work is proposed. We represent hundreds of artists precisely so the answer can be different every time.

Who handles installation, and when?

LUMAS-managed teams, scheduled around occupancy, typically floor by floor and out of guest hours. You receive a signed handover sheet per room.

Can we reorder identical works in a few years?

Your property specification is held on file, so format, framing and glazing match exactly. Editions are capped, so we flag which placements are at risk at handover and hold reserve numbers where you ask us to.

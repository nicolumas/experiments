The gallery

A quiet corner of the Hackeschen Höfe.

The Hackeschen Höfe are a working, walking place — the S-Bahn overhead, artisans in the courtyards, restaurants around the fountain. Our gallery sits inside Hof II.

Your visit

A gallery you can wander into.

Six things that stay the same, whichever LUMAS you visit.

Browse in peace.

Prices on every work. No one hovers, no one pressures. Take your afternoon.

Hear the stories.

Your director knows every edition, every artist, every hand-finish. Ask anything.

See it on your wall.

Bring a photo of your wall. We’ll mock up any work at the right size before you commit.

Take it home today.

Most editions in stock at the gallery. Hand-delivered if you prefer, across most of Europe.

Walk-through on request.

Prefer a proper tour? Book a slot with the director and we’ll walk you through what’s on.

60 days to live with it.

Hang it. Sit with it. If it doesn’t fit, return it to any LUMAS gallery, no questions asked.

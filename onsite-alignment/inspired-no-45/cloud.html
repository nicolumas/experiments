About this prototype

A magazine you can walk through.

This environment is built from the autumn issue of Inspired: the texts, works and imagery come from the layout as it stands. The darkness is not a dark mode, it is the room. The works are the light in it.

Drag to move left, right, up and down. Scroll to go deeper in and back out. Clicking a work opens its story as a constellation around the work, which you keep moving through. M shows the chapters, L opens the same issue linear and still, I shows this text.

On attribution: the layout still carries placeholder captions in many places. Works are credited here by name only where the running text of the spread names the artist unambiguously. All the others keep the magazine's own line. Nothing was invented.

Single room views: montages of photography and AI-generated picture parts, manually finished. The works themselves are unchanged.
